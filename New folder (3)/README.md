# Freelancer Portfolio Website

A modern personal portfolio site for a freelancer specializing in web development, AI tools, and automation.

## What’s included

- Hero section with outcome-focused headline and strong CTA
- About section with business problem framing
- Services section split into Web Development, AI Solutions, and Automation
- Projects section with measurable results
- Tech stack section for credibility
- Why Choose Me section with business-focused differentiators
- Testimonials section with sample client feedback
- Contact section with email and LinkedIn
- Responsive layout with light/dark theme toggle

## Design details

### Color palette
- Primary: `#4f46e5`
- Accent: `#38bdf8`
- Background dark: `#090b10`
- Surface: `rgba(20, 25, 35, 0.95)`
- Text light: `#f8fafc`
- Text muted: `#94a3b8`

### Font combination
- Primary font: `Inter`
- Fallbacks: `system-ui, sans-serif`

### Wireframe idea

1. Fixed top navigation with Brand, section links, and CTA
2. Full-width hero with headline, subheadline, CTA buttons, and outcome card
3. About section with short intro and bullet-style problem/benefit items
4. Services grid with three cards, each showing Problem → Solution → Result
5. Projects grid with 3 strong case studies and impact statements
6. Tech stack grid for quick credibility
7. Why Choose Me section with fast delivery, communication, problem-solving, business focus
8. Testimonials card row
9. Contact section with email, LinkedIn, and CTA button
